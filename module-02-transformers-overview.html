Transformers

The HV-to-LV conversion point, and a common site of confusion for HV-trained engineers new to data centres.

LessonOverviewSources & Alternative SourcesFailure Modes & IsolationConsequences & RestorationTraining StrategyKnowledge Bank

Purpose

The conversion point between HV and the LV world APs and SAPs operate in day to day — and a common site of confusion for HV-trained engineers, because LV-side protection philosophy differs sharply from DNO practice.

Normal State

A delta-star or similar winding arrangement steps HV down to LV distribution voltage. Transformers are typically paired one-for-one, or built in N+1 arrangements, with the LV switchboards they feed.
