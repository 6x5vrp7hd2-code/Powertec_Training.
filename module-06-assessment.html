Module 06 Assessment

Stored Energy — Capacitors and Batteries

Five multiple-choice questions followed by five written questions. Answers are hidden until you continue to the review page.

Part A — Multiple Choice

QUESTION 1

What is the formula for energy stored in a capacitor?

QUESTION 2

Why is a capacitor’s discharge time deliberately slow (several minutes)?

QUESTION 3

Why does a battery remain dangerous even after all AC sources to a UPS are opened and locked?

QUESTION 4

What is the only way to fully isolate a UPS battery?

QUESTION 5

What additional hazard do lithium-ion batteries introduce compared to VRLA batteries?

Part B — Written Understanding

WRITTEN QUESTION 1

Explain why the manufacturer’s discharge time is described as “a necessary condition, not a proof,” and describe the correct verification procedure that follows it.

WRITTEN QUESTION 2

Using the formula E = ½CU², explain why doubling the DC bus voltage has a much larger effect on stored energy than doubling the capacitance, and why this matters for UPS safety.

WRITTEN QUESTION 3

Describe, step by step, why “opening all the AC inputs of a UPS” does not make the battery circuit safe, and what must be done in addition.

WRITTEN QUESTION 4

Explain why shorting a charged capacitor with an uninsulated tool is dangerous, and what the correct alternative is if a discharge tool is needed.

WRITTEN QUESTION 5

Compare the specific risks (voltage, arc behaviour, failure mode) of a UPS’s capacitor bus versus its battery string.
