Module 15 Assessment

The Regulatory Framework and Electrical Qualifications

Five multiple-choice questions followed by five written questions. Answers are hidden until you continue to the review page.

Part A — Multiple Choice

QUESTION 1

What is the fundamental principle common to every regulatory framework the guide describes (French, UK, North American, and others)?

QUESTION 2

In the French NF C 18-510 system, what does the letter “C” (as in BC or HC) specifically designate?

QUESTION 3

What did the NFPA 70E 2024 edition specifically clarify regarding closed panel doors?

QUESTION 4

Under NFPA 70E, can the Incident Energy Analysis method and the Table (Category) method be mixed for the same piece of equipment?

QUESTION 5

What is a “titre d’habilitation” under the French NF C 18-510 system?

Part B — Written Understanding

WRITTEN QUESTION 1

Explain why the guide states that “the texts never replace site rules,” and describe what additional layer of authorisation is always required beyond a national/regional qualification.

WRITTEN QUESTION 2

Using the French symbol system, explain the difference between a BR (LV general intervention officer) and a BC/HC (isolation officer), including what each is and is not authorised to do.

WRITTEN QUESTION 3

Describe the 2024 updates to NFPA 70E and the related OSHA rule, and explain their practical significance for a data centre operations team.

WRITTEN QUESTION 4

Explain why a “higher qualification includes the lower levels of the same domain” (e.g., a B2 can do what a B1 does) but “a B never includes an H.” What is the reasoning behind this rule?

WRITTEN QUESTION 5

Why does the guide emphasise a “country overview for a global role,” and what practical risk does it protect against for someone working across multiple countries’ data centres?
