Module 08 Assessment

Reading a Single Line Diagram

Five multiple-choice questions followed by five written questions. Answers are hidden until you continue to the review page.

Part A — Multiple Choice

QUESTION 1

What does the perpendicular bar symbol at a switching device’s fixed contact represent, per IEC 60617?

QUESTION 2

What combination of symbols indicates a circuit breaker “suitable for isolation”?

QUESTION 3

Why must the legend of a site’s single line diagram always be confirmed before a manoeuvre, according to the guide?

QUESTION 4

What does the tag “QF2-RECT” indicate, following the described tagging convention?

QUESTION 5

What do chevrons on either side of a breaker symbol indicate?

Part B — Written Understanding

WRITTEN QUESTION 1

Explain the “additive” logic of IEC 60617 switching device symbols: how do the bar, circle, cross, and chevron marks combine to describe a device’s full capability?

WRITTEN QUESTION 2

Why does the guide insist that “doubt about a symbol is cleared BEFORE the manoeuvre, never during”? What could happen if this rule is ignored?

WRITTEN QUESTION 3

Describe the meaning and purpose of a device tag such as “QF2-RECT,” and explain why using the exact tag matters more than a description like “the breaker on the left.”

WRITTEN QUESTION 4

Compare IEC and ANSI single line diagram conventions in general terms, and explain the practical risk of a specialist assuming one convention when the site actually uses the other.

WRITTEN QUESTION 5

Why is knowing how to read a single line diagram described as “an absolute prerequisite” for understanding interlocks and MOPs?
